# Inner Archive Life Knowledge Repo

## Core Thought

Jason's private life notes are not only diary entries. They can be short mood
notes, long reflections, partial thoughts, viewpoints, event reactions,
relationship meaning, and recurring life themes. The useful architecture is
therefore a private Life Knowledge Repo, not a conventional diary.

## First Principle

The scarce resource is:

```text
expressive fidelity + privacy + future retrievability + emotional continuity + low-friction capture
```

The planning repo should not become a private diary warehouse. Planning should
keep only the locator, architecture decision, capacity impact, and next gate.
The future `inner-archive` repo should own the private raw notes.

## Recommended Repo Shape

```text
inner-archive/
  inbox/
  journal/
  thoughts/
  essays/
  people/
  events/
  themes/
  maps/
  templates/
  _attachments/
```

The starting rule is simple: capture first, organize later. The archive should
begin with real notes, not a perfect taxonomy.

## AI Agent Boundary

AI agents can help by:

- turning voice/text into Markdown;
- preserving raw text exactly;
- suggesting titles, types, tags, links, and folders;
- detecting recurring motifs;
- building weekly reflections and monthly theme maps.

AI agents should not overwrite raw emotion, flatten the voice into generic
self-help prose, decide the final meaning of an experience, or publish/share
private material without explicit approval.

Core rule:

```text
raw 永遠不可覆蓋；AI 可以整理，但不能洗掉原始的自己。
```

## Link Rules

Create a connection when:

- two notes share a concrete life theme;
- one note is an event and another is the reflection;
- a thought appears three or more times and deserves a theme page;
- a later note reinterprets an earlier note;
- a note changes future action, capacity, project direction, or a relationship
  decision;
- two notes disagree in a useful way and reveal growth, tension, or a changed
  view.

Avoid links that exist only because two notes share a broad tag.

## PCA SDD v0.1 Addendum

Jason later sharpened the design into `Personal Cognitive Archive (PCA)`.

The key distinction:

```text
diary = records what happened
PCA = records how experience becomes meaning
```

Core pipeline:

```text
Event
-> Experience
-> Interpretation
-> Belief
-> Identity
-> Later reinterpretation
```

PCA should support:

- full life records;
- thought evolution history;
- identity-growth trajectory;
- AI companion workflows;
- inner companionship;
- psychological resilience;
- future-self continuity;
- wisdom extraction.

Planning SDD:

```text
planning-everything-track/data/projects/2026-06-inner-archive-life-knowledge-repo/pca-sdd-v0.1.md
```

The SDD records system overview, non-goals, psychological / philosophical
design layer, data models, knowledge graph, resilience engine, self-companion
layer, AI agent architecture, validation metrics, failure modes, and roadmap.

The important scientific boundary is that PCA can use narrative psychology,
CBT-inspired reframing, meaning-making, post-traumatic-growth-inspired
reflection, future-self continuity, and resilience reporting as design
constructs, but it should not claim to provide therapy, diagnosis, or final
authority over Jason's life meaning.

The strongest design principle:

```text
PCA preserves the moment when life becomes meaning.
```
