## Idea Boundary

`brainstorming-lab` may explore finance-system design ideas, but it does not
store live personal finance data.

Allowed here:

- monthly review question design
- privacy-preserving analytics ideas
- decision-quality frameworks
- automation concepts before implementation
- behavioral guardrail ideas

Not allowed here:

- account balances
- account identifiers
- statements
- raw transactions
- holdings
- tax IDs
- credentials

## Canonical Split

```text
planning-everything-track = capacity / status / next gate
brainstorming-lab = idea shaping / critique / future extensions
personal-finance-os = finance records / policies / reviews / decisions
```
